# EduBot — Question Bank

A complete set of questions you can ask **EduBot**, the admission assistant for
**St. Anthony's College, Shillong**. Questions are grouped by the topics the bot is
designed to handle (admissions, programmes, eligibility, fees, departments, faculty,
hostel, exams, facilities, contact, and more).

> Tip: EduBot answers **only** from the available college resources. If something
> isn't in those resources, it will say so politely instead of guessing.

---

### Notes on how EduBot will respond
- **Grounded answers** include a direct answer, supporting details, and a helpful next step.
- **If a programme isn't offered** (e.g., BCA, B.Tech, MBA), EduBot will say it can't verify it
  and offer to list the programmes the college actually offers — it will **not** invent fees,
  eligibility, or admission requirements.
- **If information isn't available**, EduBot will politely ask you to rephrase or suggest related
  topics instead of giving a dead-end "not found" reply.
